### Example Co - 2026-05-03

Sam, thanks for raising it. This one is a limit rather than a fault: 25MB is the ceiling most mail providers accept, so anything larger would be rejected downstream even if we let you attach it. We're leaving it as it is and marking this declined, but a link-sharing option is on the roadmap and would solve the same problem properly.
