## 4. What is not in this edition

The free edition has the whole board, the whole design system in your own
brand colour, unlimited products, your own domain, and no account required

What it does not have is the part that does the work for you: the automatic
first reply, the notification emails, the embeddable widget, the agent
installer and the update pull requests. Nothing here is crippled or
time-limited. There is simply less of it.
